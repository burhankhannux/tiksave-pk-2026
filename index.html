How it works

Three simple steps — in this first version, only link validation is active.

STEP 01

Copy a video link

Open TikTok and copy the link for a video you have permission to save.

STEP 02

Paste it here

Paste the link into the field above and choose “Check link”.

STEP 03

Connect a permitted downloader

A real download option needs a compatible, policy-compliant backend. It is not connected yet.

A note about downloads

TikTok availability and download permissions vary by video and platform rules. A production version should use authorized methods, protect user privacy, and never ask for TikTok passwords or session cookies.
